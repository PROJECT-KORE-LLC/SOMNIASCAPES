# 🌙 SOMNIASCAPES

**The vibe machine you didn’t know you needed.**

SOMNIASCAPES is a tiny, chaos‑powered atmosphere engine. It is part cozy, part aesthetic, part prank, and part “why is this so relaxing?” 

You build scenes. Mix sounds. Add particles. Make a whole mood. It works like a dream‑OS: weird, pretty, slightly unhinged, and way too fun.

No ads. No subscriptions. No loopholes. Just vibes. ✨

---

## 🔮 What You Can Do
* **Build Ambient Scenes:** Mix visuals, 14+ soundscapes, particle engines, and screen glows. Make cozy rooms, aesthetic wallpapers, or cursed chaos.
* **Prank the TV:** Cast a ridiculous, chaotic scene to your living room TV to thoroughly confuse your housemates.
* **Game Night Ready:** Pop open the built-in **Scoreboard** to track infinite teams during game nights, all while your custom vibe plays in the background.
* **The Scratchpad:** Keep track of game rules, grocery lists, or midnight thoughts. It auto-saves directly to your device.
* **Save & Share:** Keep your whole vibe library forever in the local Vault, or copy a share link to instantly beam your exact creation to a friend.

## 🔒 Privacy First (Seriously.)
There are no servers. There is no cloud. There are no tracking scripts. 

SomniaScapes uses your browser's local `IndexedDB` and `localStorage` to save your data directly to your hard drive. What happens in the vault, stays in the vault.
