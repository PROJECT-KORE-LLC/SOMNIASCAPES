TUNE REALM

WORLD GUIDE

SomniaScapes is a desktop dream-city: rooms, thresholds, sound, notes, rituals, World XP, and lore.

What this is

A local-first gothic world map. Scroll the districts, open gates, use rooms, collect World XP, and uncover the city’s story over time.

How to start

Enter the city, scroll until a district calls to you, then click one gate. Do one small thing. Return when the room is finished with you.

The Castellan

The Castellan is the city’s threshold keeper. He gives lore, warnings, and plain guidance. He is lore and guidance, not an outside service, and needs no key.

World XP

World XP is the city remembering your rituals. It can later unlock gates, games, rooms, and fragments of the larger story.

Ritual games

Ink Catch is only the first visible ritual. More districts can hold their own small games: sorting, catching, feeding, finding, repairing, and remembering.

Sound safety

Use Silence All if sound stacks or a browser misbehaves. Use Restore Sound to wake the last ambience again when the city is quiet.

“SomniaScapes is a city built from abandoned dreams, repaired memories, and little rituals of survival.”
