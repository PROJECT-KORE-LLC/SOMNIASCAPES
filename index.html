NEXUS SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

1. Nexus Map Guide

SomniaScapes is a living world of rooms. Each room holds a real-life tool: time, meals, cleaning, writing, learning, rest, records, and home care. The Nexus is the map between them.

  • Enter a room when you need support for that part of life.
  • Each room has its own atmosphere, audio, companions, and tools.
  • Changing the atmosphere is part of the app, not decoration.
  • XP is earned for showing up, interacting, tending, resting, returning, and trying again.
  • Room XP unlocks and evolves companions.
  • World XP unlocks sealed ??? encounters and mini-games.
  • There are no shame loops. Missed days do not erase progress.
  • The world changes with you.

2. Circadian Rhythm & Environment

The Nexus follows a gentle circadian cycle. Time of day, weather, moonlight, and ambient audio shift the world's mood automatically. Use Tune the Realm for quick overrides or comfort adjustments.

3. Awakening the Sovereign Oracle

To connect the Castellan terminal, add a Gemini API key to enable Oracle features. Your key is stored in this browser’s local storage.

4. World Backups

Clicking the "World Snapshot" button currently exports your Nexus Master Ledger. Room-specific backups remain inside their individual rooms. Because SomniaScapes runs completely client-side, executing periodic backups is a safe way to preserve your progress, timelines, and records locally.

5. Sealed Encounters

The hidden ??? doors scattered on the map frame specific hidden mini-games and dynamic world events. These zones break open as your global cumulative World XP values advance across task completion tracks.
